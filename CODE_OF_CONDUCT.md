# CocoaPDF community conduct

CocoaPDF welcomes technically rigorous, good-faith participation. Be
respectful, discuss evidence rather than people, and make space for different
levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, or other
project spaces.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the project and its participants. Enforcement decisions will
consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

For a repository concern that is safe to discuss publicly, open an issue with
only the minimum necessary detail. For threats, targeted harassment, or other
platform-safety concerns that should remain private, use GitHub's **Report
abuse or spam** flow. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.
